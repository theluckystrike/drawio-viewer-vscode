# Shape details

Click a shape or a connector to see:

- its label as plain text, its tooltip and its link (as text: the viewer never opens it),
- its shape name, layer, position and size, or for a connector the shapes at both ends,
- every custom data field stored on it (Edit Data in the diagram editor).

Drag the canvas to pan. Ctrl or Cmd and the mouse wheel zoom at the pointer. F fits the page, 0 shows it at 100 %.
