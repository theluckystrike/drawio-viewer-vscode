# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release.
- Opens .drawio and .dio diagrams read-only: plain and compressed XML, every page, with page tabs, zoom, pan, fit, layers, find across pages and a light or dark canvas.
- Click a shape or connector to read its label, tooltip, link (as text) and custom data. Broken files show a clear message.
- Zovo Pro tools, 1 free try each: Export SVG and PNG, Compare diagrams (Markdown report), Extract labels and data (CSV or Markdown), Batch export a folder to SVG.
